## 1. Project Overview

Ad-Vocates is a downloadable, privacy focused ad blocking application designed to detect and block advertisements, pop-ups, trackers, and suspicious web requests across commonly used websites and search engines.

The application will include a user friendly dashboard that displays useful diagnostics, including advertisements blocked, estimated data saved, and browsing activity. Users will also be able to customize protection settings, whitelist trusted websites, and receive automatic filter updates.

The goal of Ad-Vocates is to create a reliable and efficient tool that improves browsing performance while giving users greater privacy, security, and control over their web experience.

## 7. Decision-Making Process

The team will attempt to make project decisions through discussion and mutual agreement.

Because Ad-Vocates consists of two team members, both members will be given the opportunity to explain their reasoning before a significant technical, design, or project-management decision is made.

For routine decisions, the member primarily responsible for the relevant area may make reasonable decisions while keeping the other team member informed.

For significant decisions involving project scope, architecture, major features, deadlines, security, privacy, or project requirements, both members should agree before the change is implemented.

If the team cannot reach an agreement after discussing the available options, the team will consult the faculty advisor or course instructor for guidance rather than allowing the disagreement to delay the project.

---

## 8. Conflict Resolution

Team members agree to address disagreements professionally and directly.

If a disagreement occurs, the team will use the following process:

1. Discussing the issue directly and explaining individual concerns and proposed solutions.
2. Both members will consider relevant evidence, including project requirements, technical feasibility, project deadlines, testing results, user needs, and security or privacy concerns.
3. The team will attempt to reach a mutually acceptable solution.
4. If the disagreement cannot be resolved internally within a reasonable amount of time, the team will request guidance from the faculty advisor.
5. If necessary, unresolved issues affecting the completion of course requirements may be brought to the course instructor.

The purpose of this process is to resolve conflicts based on what best supports the successful completion of the Ad-Vocates project.

## 9. Accountability and Work Expectations

Both team members are expected to make consistent and meaningful contributions throughout the two semester Senior Design project.

Each team member is responsible for completing agreed-upon assignments by their expected deadlines.

If a team member believes that an assigned task cannot be completed on time, that member should notify the other team member as early as possible and explain the problem.

If project responsibilities become unbalanced, the team will discuss the workload and redistribute tasks when reasonable.

If a pattern of missed responsibilities develops, the following process will be used:

1. The issue will first be discussed directly between both team members.
2. Responsibilities and expectations will be clarified.
3. The team will establish a reasonable plan for completing missed or delayed work.
4. Continued problems will be documented when necessary.
5. If the problem cannot be resolved internally, the faculty advisor or course instructor will be consulted.

Both team members recognize that their individual workloads may vary depending on the current stage of the project, but the overall workload should remain reasonably balanced over the duration of the project.

## 10. Project Quality and Professional Expectations

The Ad-Vocates team will strive to produce a reliable, maintainable, and professionally documented software application.

Because the project focuses on privacy, security, and control over web browsing, the team will consider these concerns when making development and testing decisions.

The team will make reasonable efforts to:

* Avoid introducing unnecessary security or privacy risks.
* Test important blocking and filtering functionality before considering features complete.
* Maintain understandable and organized source code.
* Document important design decisions and known limitations.
* Evaluate the performance impact of blocking and filtering functionality.
* Ensure that customization and whitelisting features behave as intended.
* Follow applicable Senior Design course requirements throughout development.
