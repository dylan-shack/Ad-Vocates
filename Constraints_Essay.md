**Ad-Vocates Project Constraints Essay**  
Team Members: Dylan Shackleford, Max Marsh  
**Security**  
Security is the primary goal of the Ad-Vocates project. The goal of the project is to create a robust blocker that block trackers, suspicious web requests and protects users privacy while browsing. The dashboard and statistics that estimate data saved and blocked advertisements will be calculated with minimal data collection to ensure that the goal of user privacy is not undermined. Automatically updating filters also must involve verification of sources before applying changes to ensure a secure platform.  
**Ethical**  
For this project, ethical factors must be considered due to the customizable protection settings of the ad-blocker. This feature needs to make protection choices clear to the user, since whitelisting a page would potentially allow advertisements and redirects that would otherwise be blocked. As well, the estimated data savings must be clearly labeled as an estimate, since the data is not exact and should not be presented as such.  
**Social**  
Social factors must be considered since the goal of the application is to block advertisements and redirects on the web. This will enhance users’ quality of life by reducing interruptions while browsing. However, blocking pop-ups and redirects can negatively affect the usability of websites, so there is a trade-off between privacy and protection versus website usability. Allowing for customizable settings can balance the constraint and conflict of privacy versus usability by allowing users to decide the protection levels.
